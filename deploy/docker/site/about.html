About

Northwind Data Systems is a four-person studio. We take on a small number of engagements at a time, usually with operations teams who have outgrown their spreadsheets but do not need a new platform.

What we do

Import and reconciliation pipelines, internal dashboards, and the reporting glue between systems that were never designed to talk to each other.

What we do not do

We do not resell licences, we do not run a hosted service for you, and we do not disappear after the invoice. Everything we build is deployed into your accounts and handed over with documentation.
